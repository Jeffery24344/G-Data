# Privacy Policy for G Data

**Effective date:** 26 August 2026  
**Publisher / Developer:** Big Big Dream  
**Application:** G Data – Smart Data Saver & Network Optimizer

---

## 1. Introduction

Big Big Dream (“we”, “us”, “our”) develops and publishes the Android application **G Data**.  
This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, how it is used, and the choices you have.

G Data is designed with a **privacy-first, local-first** approach. The goal is to help you reduce unnecessary mobile-data consumption while collecting as little information as possible.

---

## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1 Information processed on your device (local only)

The app uses official Android system APIs to read:

- Mobile data and (where relevant) Wi-Fi data usage totals
- Per-application data consumption
- Connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile)
- Battery level and basic network state (used only for smart optimization rules)
- Data-bundle information that **you manually enter** (total size, remaining amount, expiry)

These statistics are stored **locally on your device** using Android’s Room database and DataStore. They are used to:

- Show usage dashboards and charts
- Calculate estimated savings
- Power automatic optimization rules
- Display recommendations

### 2.2 Information we do **not** collect

G Data does **not** collect, transmit, or store:

- Passwords or login credentials
- Private messages, emails, or chat content
- Banking, payment, or financial information
- Personal files or media
- Authentication tokens
- Browsing history or the content of websites you visit
- Precise location
- Any data obtained by decrypting HTTPS / TLS traffic

The optional local VPN feature (when enabled by you) operates as a pass-through or limited traffic-management tunnel. It does **not** decrypt encrypted traffic and does not send your traffic content to our servers.

### 2.3 Permissions

| Permission                    | Purpose                                      |
|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Usage Access (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`) | Read data-usage statistics from Android |
| Notifications                 | Useful alerts (daily limits, high usage, bundle expiry) |
| VPN service                   | Optional local optimization (user must explicitly approve) |
| Foreground service            | Required by Android while the optional VPN is active |

You can revoke Usage Access or VPN permission at any time in system Settings. Doing so limits or disables the corresponding features.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

All usage statistics and settings are processed **on your device** for the following purposes only:

- Displaying data-usage and savings information to you
- Applying the optimization mode you selected (Performance / Balanced / Extreme)
- Running smart rules (e.g. low battery, low remaining data)
- Showing notifications you have allowed
- Improving the on-device experience of the app

We do **not** sell your data.  
We do **not** use the data for advertising profiling.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

- Statistics and preferences are stored only on your device.
- You can delete all locally stored statistics at any time from the in-app **Privacy** screen.
- Uninstalling G Data removes the app’s local data (subject to normal Android behaviour).

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

In the current version, G Data does **not** send usage statistics or personal data to external analytics, advertising, or cloud services for the core data-saving functionality.

If future versions introduce optional cloud features (e.g. anonymous crash reporting), this Privacy Policy will be updated and you will be informed.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

G Data is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 7. Security

We implement reasonable technical measures consistent with a local-first Android application:

- No unnecessary transmission of data
- Use of official Android APIs and security model
- No attempt to bypass system protections or decrypt secure traffic

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You use the app at your own risk.

---

## 8. Your Choices

- Grant or deny **Usage Access** in system Settings.
- Grant or deny **notification** permission.
- Approve or revoke the **VPN** permission at any time.
- Turn optimization off completely inside the app.
- Delete all local statistics from the Privacy screen.
- Uninstall the application.
